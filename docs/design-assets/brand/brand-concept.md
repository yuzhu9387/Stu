# Baby-mouth brand concept

Date: 2026-09-17

Status: visual and naming exploration; a final name has not been selected.

## Logo

Asset: [baby-mouth-logo-concept.png](./baby-mouth-logo-concept.png)

A rounded open baby mouth with two little lower milk teeth and a small peach tongue. Sage green and warm ivory connect the mark to the current kitchen workspace. Keep the silhouette simple and readable at app-icon sizes. This PNG is a concept image, not a production vector asset.

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Original generation and refined exports remain in the Codex generated_images directory.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. A very cute ultra-minimal flat graphic logo of a laughing BABY MOUTH ONLY for a family meal app. Important: it must immediately read as a MOUTH, NOT a creature face. Use a classic soft wide D-shaped open smile: a gently curved almost-horizontal upper edge and a rounded U-shaped bottom, smooth rounded corners with NO pointed or raised lobes. Single solid muted dark sage mouth cavity. Exactly TWO TINY rounded ivory baby teeth protrude UPWARD from the BOTTOM edge close together just left of center; the rest of the gums are empty. NO upper teeth at all. A tiny peach tongue shape sits inside at lower RIGHT, well away from the two lower teeth. Mouth width about 1.6x mouth height. Sweet, silly, toddler just growing their first two bottom teeth, ready to eat. Strong simple silhouette with only four uniform flat filled vector-style shapes. Cream #FAF7F1 background; sage #466249 mouth; ivory #FFF9ED teeth; peach #E9A188 tongue. Square composition, single centered mark, 60% canvas width, large clean empty margins. Sharp clean graphic contours and purely opaque solid colors. Absolutely NO eyes, nose, cheeks, lips, full face, head, eyes disguised as teeth, horns, monsters, scary expression, red lipstick, dentistry symbols, gradients, texture, shadows, bevels, 3D effects, text, letters, wordmarks or decorative elements. Do not make a brand presentation sheet; one final logo mark only.

## Final refinement prompt

Refine this baby-mouth logo into clean flat graphic artwork. Preserve the exact mouth shape, two little bottom teeth and peach tongue placement. Replace every mottled, translucent or shaded green pixel with ONE uniform completely opaque solid sage green #466249. Teeth are opaque ivory #FFF9ED. Tongue is opaque peach #E9A188. Entire square background is solid warm cream #FAF7F1, fully opaque, edge to edge, no transparent area. No texture, no gradients, no light reflections, no shading, no thin white outline, no noise. Clean smooth vector-style edges. Single mark centered with generous empty margins. Mouth and teeth must be pure uniform flat colors like a professional minimal app logo. No additional elements or text. This is a flat 2D logo printed on cream paper, NOT a 3D rendering.
